Common questions

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Filemon comes out, looks at the job in person and gives you a written estimate. There is no charge for the visit.

Where do you work?

About 50 miles around North Augusta, SC, on both sides of the Savannah River. Not sure you are in range? Call and ask.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. The phone is answered in English or Spanish, and you can choose your language on the estimate form.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards. Through PayPal you can also split the cost over time — interest-free installments on smaller jobs, or monthly financing on larger projects. Terms and eligibility are set by PayPal and shown before you commit.
