About Us

15+ Years in the Field. FPC Construction LLC, Est. 2020.

Filemon brings over 15 years of hands-on experience in the field. FPC Construction LLC was established in 2020 and serves North Augusta, SC and the surrounding CSRA. What started as a heavy-equipment and site-work operation has grown into a full construction outfit — with septic and drainage as our specialty.

Our team of experienced professionals is committed to delivering quality workmanship on every project, big or small. We take pride in transforming our clients' visions into reality while maintaining the highest standards of safety and craftsmanship.
