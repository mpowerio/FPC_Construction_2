About our text message program

FPC Construction LLC ("FPC Construction," "we," "us") sends text messages to customers and prospective customers who have agreed to receive them. Messages are sent from our business number (803) 884-9305.

What we send

  • Your estimate when it is ready, with a link to view and accept it from your phone
  • Appointment confirmations and scheduling after you request an estimate visit
  • Replies and updates about your own job, such as scheduling changes or answers to your questions

We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency varies with your project. Message and data rates may apply.

How customers opt in

When you speak with FPC Construction, whether with our AI voice assistant or with a member of our team, on the phone or in person, you can give us your mobile number and ask for your estimate or updates by text. We ask for your permission to text you, and we send texts only after you agree.

Submitting the contact form on our website does not sign you up for text messages.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help, call us at (803) 884-9305, or email info@fpcconstructions.com.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are delivered through service providers acting on our behalf. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
