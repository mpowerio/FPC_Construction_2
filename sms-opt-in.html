SMS Program Terms & Opt-In Policy — FPC Construction

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who sends these texts.

FPC Construction LLC ("FPC Construction," "we," "us") sends text messages from our business number (803) 884-9305. FPC Construction is the sender of every message, and each message names FPC Construction.

Two separate text programs. You choose each one.

1. Appointment & Estimate Texts.

Texts about a request you made:

  • confirmation that we received your request;
  • your booked visit time, and a reminder the day before your visit;
  • your estimate when it is ready, with a link to view and accept it;
  • follow-ups about your estimate (for example, "any questions?" and "should we keep this estimate open?");
  • your invoice link, and replies to messages you send us.

Frequency varies with your project; usually a few texts per job.

2. Offers & Updates.

Marketing texts such as seasonal offers, maintenance check-ins, and photos of similar jobs we recently finished. Up to 4 messages per month.

How you opt in.

  • Website: on our estimate form or booking page there are two separate checkboxes, one per program. Both start unchecked, and both are optional. You can submit the form and get an estimate without checking either one.
  • Phone: when you call (803) 884-9305, an FPC team member or our AI phone assistant may ask: "Is it OK if FPC texts you at this number about your estimate and appointment? It's usually just a few texts per job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop anytime." This covers Appointment & Estimate Texts only. We never sign you up for Offers & Updates over the phone.
  • In person: you can sign a short paper form with the same two separate checkboxes.
  • By text: text OFFERS (or OFERTAS) to (803) 884-9305 to join Offers & Updates. We reply once to confirm.

We record when and how you agreed, and the exact words you agreed to.

Consent is not a condition of purchase.

You never have to agree to texts to get an estimate, book a visit, or buy anything from us.

Cost.

Msg & data rates may apply.

Opt out or get help.

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts from us. You will get one text confirming you are unsubscribed, then no more texts unless you sign up again. We also honor other clear requests to stop, such as "unsubscribe," "cancel," "quit," "end," "revoke" or "opt out," and in Spanish "alto," "parar" or "cancelar," or a call or email asking us to stop.
  • Reply HELP for help, call (803) 884-9305, or email fpineda@fpcconstructions.com.

Quiet hours.

We do not send scheduled texts late at night or early in the morning. Replies to a message you just sent us may come at any time.

Carriers

are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy.

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in data and consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Messages are delivered through a text-message service provider acting on our behalf.

See our Privacy Policy and the text message section of our Terms of Service.
