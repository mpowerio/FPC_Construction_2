SMS Program Terms & Opt-In Policy — FPC Construction

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who sends these texts.

FPC Construction LLC ("FPC Construction," "we," "us") sends text messages from our business number (803) 884-9305. FPC Construction is the sender of every message, and each message names FPC Construction.

Current service and planned expansion

Today, our text-message program covers your request acknowledgment and estimate link after you consent. The expanded appointment, reminder, follow-up and Offers & Updates programs described below are planned and are not open for enrollment yet. Publishing these terms does not sign you up or start the new programs.

Planned programs: separate choices

1. Appointment & Estimate Texts.

Texts about a request you made:

  • confirmation that we received your request;
  • your booked visit time, and a reminder the day before your visit;
  • your estimate when it is ready, with a link to view and accept it;
  • follow-ups about your estimate (for example, "any questions?" and "should we keep this estimate open?");
  • your invoice link, and replies to messages you send us.

Frequency varies with your project; usually a few texts per job.

2. Offers & Updates.

Marketing texts such as seasonal offers, maintenance check-ins, and photos of similar jobs we recently finished. Up to 4 messages per month.

How you opt in.

  • Website: Website text-message signup is not available yet. When it becomes available, our estimate and booking forms will offer two separate, optional checkboxes, one per program. Both will start unchecked. Submitting today's estimate form does not sign you up for texts.
  • Phone: when you call (803) 884-9305, an FPC team member or our AI phone assistant may ask: "Is it OK if FPC texts you at this number about your estimate and appointment? It's usually just a few texts per job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop anytime." This currently covers request acknowledgment and estimate-link texts only. We never sign you up for Offers & Updates over the phone.
  • In person: For current request and estimate messages, you can ask an FPC team member in person. A signed paper form with two separate choices for the planned programs is not available yet.
  • By text: OFFERS/OFERTAS enrollment is not available yet. We plan to offer keyword signup and a confirmation message when the new programs launch.

Our current records include consent information recorded for request and estimate messages. Before the expanded programs launch, we plan to add a consent ledger recording the program, when and how you agreed, and the exact wording shown. Online signup and its consent records are not available yet. When online signup becomes available, that record will also include the IP address and browser information sent with the form. We also keep message history, replies, delivery status, and STOP or HELP requests.

Consent is not a condition of purchase.

You never have to agree to texts to get an estimate, book a visit, or buy anything from us.

Cost.

Msg & data rates may apply.

Opt out or get help.

Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. You can also call (803) 884-9305 or email fpineda@fpcconstructions.com to ask us to stop or to get help. Please use STOP, a call or email rather than relying on other keywords. Support for additional opt-out keywords is planned for the expanded programs; it is not represented as available here. Signing up again will require a new consent process; replying START alone does not enroll you.

Quiet hours.

Before the expanded scheduled-message programs launch, we will apply a quiet-hours rule. Those scheduled programs are not active yet. Replies to a message you just sent us may come at any time.

Carriers

are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy.

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in data and consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Messages are delivered through a text-message service provider acting on our behalf.

See our Privacy Policy and the text message section of our Terms of Service.
