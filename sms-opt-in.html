About our text message program

Program name: FPC Construction Customer Texts. FPC Construction LLC ("FPC Construction," "we," "us") sends text messages to customers and prospective customers who have agreed to receive them. Messages are sent from our business number (803) 884-9305. FPC Construction is the sender of every message, and each message names FPC Construction.

What we send

  • A confirmation that we received your estimate request
  • Your estimate when it is ready, with a link to view and accept it from your phone
  • Appointment confirmations, scheduling changes and other updates about your own estimate visit or job

We do not send marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency varies with your project; it is usually just a few texts per job. Msg & data rates may apply.

How customers opt in

When you call (803) 884-9305, an FPC team member or FPC’s AI phone assistant will ask: “Is it OK if FPC texts you at this number about your estimate and appointment? It's usually just a few texts per job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop anytime.” We send text messages only after you clearly say yes, and we record your consent on your customer record. You can also ask a team member in person to text you. Before you agree, the team member gives you the same information: it is usually just a few texts per job, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply HELP for help or STOP to stop anytime. Submitting the website form does not sign you up for text messages.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. You will get one text confirming that you are unsubscribed, and no more texts after that unless you opt in again, for example by replying START. Reply HELP for help, call us at (803) 884-9305, or email info@fpcconstructions.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in data and consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are delivered through a text-message service provider acting on our behalf. Our Privacy Policy is the privacy policy for this program, and the text message section of our Terms of Service contains the full program terms.
