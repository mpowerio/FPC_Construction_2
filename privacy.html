This Privacy Policy explains how FPC Construction LLC (“FPC Construction,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information when you use fpcconstructions.com, request an estimate, talk with our AI voice assistant, or exchange text messages with us. We provide land clearing, grading, privacy fencing, septic, concrete and related contractor work in North Augusta, Aiken, Augusta and the surrounding CSRA.

No sellingWe do not sell personal data.
TextsReply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
Mobile infoNever shared for marketing or promotion.

Information we collect

  • Estimate and contact form. Your name, phone number, email address, the service you need, and whatever you choose to write under project details, which may include your project address or a preferred time to meet.
  • Phone calls. Calls to our business line may be answered by an automated AI voice assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed by the voice-assistant provider that works for us. On a call we may capture your name, phone number, email, project address, project details and preferred appointment time so we can follow up.
  • Website voice widget. If you talk with the voice assistant on our website, what you say may be processed, recorded and transcribed by the same provider.
  • Text messages. Your mobile number, current consent information, message history, delivery status, and STOP or HELP requests. For the planned expanded programs, we intend to record program-specific consent and the exact wording shown. Website text-message signup is not available yet; when it becomes available, its consent record will include the IP address and browser information sent with the form.

How we use it

  • To respond to your estimate request and answer your questions.
  • To schedule site visits and appointments.
  • To send the current request and estimate messages with your consent and, after the expanded programs launch, the planned Appointment & Estimate Texts and separately chosen Offers & Updates described below, and to honor STOP and HELP requests. We use your text messaging opt-in data and consent record only for our text message program and for STOP and HELP, and we never sell them or share them for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number itself is also used to respond to your request, call you back and follow up, schedule visits and keep your customer record, and the service providers described below handle it only to help us do those things.
  • To send invoices and payment reminders by email.
  • To keep your customer record in FPC’s customer system.

Text messaging (SMS)

Today, our text-message program covers your request acknowledgment and estimate link after you consent. The expanded appointment, reminder, follow-up and Offers & Updates programs described below are planned and are not open for enrollment yet. Publishing these terms does not sign you up or start the new programs.

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in data and consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

  • Two programs, separate consent:
    • Appointment & Estimate Texts: request confirmation, visit confirmation and day-before reminder, estimate link, estimate follow-ups, invoice link and replies.
    • Offers & Updates: marketing texts such as seasonal offers, maintenance check-ins and photos of similar jobs.

You choose each one separately.

  • How you opt in:
    • Website text-message signup is not available yet. When it becomes available, our estimate and booking forms will offer two separate, optional checkboxes, one per program. Both will start unchecked. Submitting today's estimate form does not sign you up for texts.
    • by phone, for the current request acknowledgment and estimate-link texts only;
    • For current request and estimate messages, you can ask an FPC team member in person. A signed paper form with two separate choices for the planned programs is not available yet.

See our SMS Terms & Opt-In Policy for the exact wording.

  • What we keep as proof: Our current records include consent information recorded for request and estimate messages. Before the expanded programs launch, we plan to add a consent ledger recording the program, when and how you agreed, and the exact wording shown. Online signup and its consent records are not available yet. When online signup becomes available, that record will also include the IP address and browser information sent with the form. We also keep message history, replies, delivery status, and STOP or HELP requests.

We honor any reasonable request to stop, including a plain-language reply such as “please stop texting me,” or a request by phone or email. Signing up again will require a new consent process; replying START alone does not enroll you.
  • Consent: agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • Who sends them: FPC Construction is the sender of every message, and each message names FPC Construction. Our text-message provider uses your mobile number only to deliver our messages and process STOP and HELP.
  • Terms: full program terms are in our Terms of Service and SMS Terms & Opt-In Policy. This Privacy Policy is the privacy policy for our text message program.

Who processes information for us

We use service providers who handle information on our behalf only to run the services we ask for: voice and AI providers (the phone and website voice assistant, and AI tools in our customer system that help us prepare estimates and summaries), a text-message provider, a payment and invoicing provider (PayPal) that sends our invoices and payment-reminder emails and receives your name, email address, job address, a description of the work and the amount billed, an email provider that delivers website-form contents, voice-assistant lead notifications and the emails customers send us, and hosting and database providers for this website and our customer system. We do not sell personal data. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Our text-message provider receives your mobile number only to deliver our messages to you. Apart from that, all of the sharing described above excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent: this information will not be shared with any third parties, and no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Our website and third-party components

We have not added analytics, advertising pixels or behavioral tracking scripts to this website. Outside components that load on our home page include the voice-assistant widget from our voice provider (ElevenLabs), a message from our payment provider (PayPal) that displays financing (“Pay Later”) information, and our photo gallery, which is hosted by our storage and database provider (Supabase). Whenever the home page loads, your browser connects directly to those providers, even if you never submit the form or use the assistant. They may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and the voice and payment providers may use their own cookies under their own privacy policies. The estimate form sends what you enter to our customer system, and we use your phone number and email from it to respond to your request and to serve you as a customer.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.
  • You may use the estimate form instead of speaking with the voice assistant.
  • To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, contact us using the details below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Security

No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive information such as a Social Security number or full payment card number by text message or through the estimate form.

Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the updated version here and change the date at the top of the page. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Nuestro sitio web y componentes de terceros

No hemos agregado a este sitio web herramientas de análisis, píxeles de publicidad ni scripts de seguimiento de comportamiento. Entre los componentes externos que cargan en nuestra página principal están el asistente de voz de nuestro proveedor de voz (ElevenLabs), un mensaje de nuestro proveedor de pagos (PayPal) que muestra información de financiamiento (“Pay Later”) y nuestra galería de fotos, alojada por nuestro proveedor de almacenamiento y bases de datos (Supabase).
